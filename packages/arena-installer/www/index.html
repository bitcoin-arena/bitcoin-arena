    2. What your node does

    Where your earnings come from

    Every paid round sends 4 % of its stakes to the nodes that serve it. It is split into three equal pools, one per role:

    Example: rounds with 1,000,000 sat of stakes - 40,000 sat go to the nodes. Each seated node gets:
    Game / verify≈ 1,905 sat
    Settlement≈ 1,905 sat
    Guardian≈ 889 sat
    Gateway (on top of guardian)≈ 2,222 sat
    One node can hold several roles and earns each share. All shares are paid in the round's settlement - no claims to file.

    5. How others reach your node

    Players and other nodes connect to your node. First the installer checks whether the internet can reach this machine directly.

    Direct connection

    Your node listens on the port above. Needs the port open in the firewall and, behind a router, forwarded to this machine.

    Permanent Cloudflare tunnel

    A fixed https name such as node.example.com, through your own free Cloudflare account and a domain on it. No open ports.

    Temporary anonymous tunnel

    A random trycloudflare.com name, no account. It changes whenever the tunnel restarts, so others lose your node until it is announced again.

    How are seats chosen?

    Only the federation's own guardians can stand. Every day (144 blocks) its guardians rank the gateways that meet the three needs above - most Lightning routed first, then channel size, uptime and bond - and take the top two. While gateways are equal it comes down to a fair draw, so every eligible gateway gets its days. A seated gateway keeps its seat unless it drops out or another routes 25 % more.

    8. Your federation

    A federation is a group of 10 nodes that hold the players' bitcoin together.

    🔒 Moving any of it takes 7 of the 10. No single node - not yours either - can take it.

    ⚡ Its two gateways connect it to Lightning; every guardian earns a share of each round.

    9. Your bond: the rules

    🔒
    Your bond is your promise.

    Bitcoin your node locks on the chain. It makes cheating cost more than it could ever win - that is why players can trust a node they have never met.

    ✅
    Stopping? Press LEAVE first.

    On your owner page. Your node finishes the rounds it holds and gives notice to its federation, then you are free. Your bond comes back when its lock ends.

    ⚠️
    Just switching off - without LEAVE:

    Your bond is slashed: you lose it. A guardian that vanishes leaves its federation short, and the bitcoin it guards at risk.
