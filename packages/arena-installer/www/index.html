    5. How others reach your node

    Players and other nodes connect to your node. First the installer checks whether the internet can reach this machine directly.

    Direct connection

    Your node listens on the port above. Needs the port open in the firewall and, behind a router, forwarded to this machine.

    Permanent Cloudflare tunnel

    A fixed https name such as node.example.com, through your own free Cloudflare account and a domain on it. No open ports.

    Temporary anonymous tunnel

    A random trycloudflare.com name, no account. It changes whenever the tunnel restarts, so others lose your node until it is announced again.

    6. Install and start

    7. Lightning (custody)

    Custody needs a Lightning node. The installer can set up LND here - checked download, testnet4 on your Bitcoin Core, started as a service, its wallet made from your 24 words (one seed for everything; LND's channel backup file is separate, the owner page shows where) - or connect one you already run.

    The gateway

    The Fedimint gateway pays and receives Lightning for a federation's users through this LND. Its e-cash wallet is made from your 24 words too.

    Hosting a gateway seat

    Every federation has two gateway seats, held by two of its own guardians. If more want them, the guardians choose the best two every day (144 blocks): first by the Lightning volume each routed for the federation, then channel capacity, availability, bond, and a fair draw. A seated gateway keeps its seat unless it fails the rules or someone routes 25 % more.

    To stand (testnet4): be one of the federation's guardians (your node, once drawn), public channels of at least 200,000 sat, a peg-in of at least 50,000 sat into the federation (the bond step sets it aside; your node sends it by itself), and be up 95 % of the time.

    What it earns: one third of the federation's custody fee goes to its two gateway seats - about 0.28 % of every round's volume each, against 0.11 % for a guardian of ten - plus your Lightning routing fees.
