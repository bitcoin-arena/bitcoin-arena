    Run a Bitcoin Arena node

    Your computer helps run Bitcoin Arena: it checks the games, pays out the winners and can hold players' bitcoin with other nodes. For that it earns a share of every paid round.

    4 % of every paid round goes to the nodes, 1 % for each service, shared by the nodes doing it.
    Example: 100 rounds of 1,000 sat → 4,000 sat to the nodes. More rounds, more earnings. Testnet4 for now: its coins are for testing.

    What you need

    • 💻A computer that stays onMac, Linux or Windows
    • 💽About 3 GB of diskmore if it installs Bitcoin Core (testnet4: 25 GB, pruned 6 GB)
    • 🌐Home internetno open port needed: a tunnel or Tor is set up for you
    • 🔒A bond of 50,000 sattestnet4 coins, free from a faucet; locked for a while, then yours again

    Your node reads the testnet4 chain. Use your own Bitcoin Core, or an Electrum server.

    2. What your node does

    Where your earnings come from

    Every paid round: 4 % of its stakes to the nodes, 1 % per service.

    Example: 100 rounds of 1,000 sat → 4,000 sat to the nodes:
    Game / verify1,000 sat
    Settlement1,000 sat
    Custody1,000 sat
    Liquidity providers1,000 sat
    Each role's share is split among the nodes doing it. One node can hold several roles and earns each share.

    5. How others reach your node

    Players and other nodes connect to your node. First the installer checks whether the internet can reach this machine directly.

    Direct connection

    Your node listens on the port above. Needs the port open in the firewall and, behind a router, forwarded to this machine.

    Permanent Cloudflare tunnel

    A fixed https name such as node.example.com, through your own free Cloudflare account and a domain on it (Cloudflare sells one for a few dollars a year). No open ports. No domain? Choose workers.dev or Tailscale Funnel below.

    Permanent Tailscale Funnel

    A fixed https name such as my-pc.tail1234.ts.net from your free Tailscale account - no domain needed, no open ports. Install Tailscale and log in (tailscale up) first; Funnel must be allowed for this machine in your tailnet.

    Permanent Cloudflare name, no domain (workers.dev)

    A fixed name such as arena-node-1a2b3c.yourname.workers.dev on your own free Cloudflare account, no open ports: a small Worker there forwards to your node's tunnel and your node keeps it pointed at it. Free up to 100,000 requests a day (Workers Paid: $5 a month).

    How are seats chosen?

    Only the federation's own guardians can stand. Every day (144 blocks) its guardians rank the gateways that meet the three needs above - most Lightning routed first, then channel size, uptime and bond - and take the top two. While gateways are equal it comes down to a fair draw, so every eligible gateway gets its days. A seated gateway keeps its seat unless it drops out or another routes 25 % more.

    8. Your federation

    A federation is a group of 10 nodes that hold the players' bitcoin together.

    🔒 Moving any of it takes 7 of the 10. No single node - not yours either - can take it.

    ⚡ Its two gateways connect it to Lightning; every guardian earns a share of each round.

    How your node gets its seat - automatically, whichever comes first:

    ✨ A new federation

    Your node and 9 other new nodes are drawn to start one together.

    🔄 A seat in a successor

    When a federation shrinks (a guardian leaves), a new one takes over its players' bitcoin: its remaining guardians first, the missing seats drawn from nodes like yours.

    Nobody is invited and nobody picks: the draw is fixed by the chain, so no one can choose who guards the money. A full federation takes no new guardian.

    9. Your bond: the rules

    🔒
    Your bond is your promise.

    Bitcoin your node locks on the chain. It makes cheating cost more than it could ever win - that is why players can trust a node they have never met.

    ✅
    Stopping? Press LEAVE first.

    On your owner page. Your node finishes the rounds it holds and gives notice to its federation, then you are free. Your bond comes back when its lock ends.

    ⚠️
    Just switching off - without LEAVE:

    Your bond is slashed: you lose it. A guardian that vanishes leaves its federation short, and the bitcoin it guards at risk.
