Project improvement proposals

PIPs improve Bitcoin Arena as a whole: its games, its protocol, its tools and its community, not only the protocol. Each one asks the protocol reserves to fund a project, and anyone can write one. Read them, ask questions and discuss them here, signed in with the key and the name you play with. The bonded nodes decide.

How a proposal is decided
  1. Signal: at least 15% of the active nodes signal it within a week.
  2. Who votes: bonds at least 3 months old that stay locked at least 3 months after the vote.
  3. How much they count: bond × age: 1× under a year, 2× from one year, 3× from two.
  4. When it passes: after 2 weeks of voting, 60% of the votes say YES, and YES is at least 20% of everyone who could vote.
  5. How much it can take: at most 10% of the reserves per proposal, 25% per 3 months.
  6. Time to say no: nothing is paid for 2 weeks after the vote, and NO votes in that time stop it. Above 1% of the reserves, it is paid in 4 monthly parts, each stoppable.
