Referral Program

Bring players to Bitcoin Arena from your site. Every game they play pays you 10% of the nodes' fee. Your players pay nothing extra.

10%of the nodes' fee on every stake your players place
Same pricefor your players: your 10% comes out of the nodes' part, not their stake
Lightningto withdraw what you earned, whenever you like

An example

A player you brought stakes 10,000 sat. The nodes' fee on it is 400 sat today (4%). Your program gets 40 sat of that, the nodes keep 360. The player's stake and fee are the same as without you.

Set it up

  1. Run a node. Your program lives on your own node: it holds the program's key and collects what it earns. How to install a node.
  2. Open your node's owner area and go to Referrer. Give your program a name, for example the name of your site. You can have as many programs as you like, one per site or campaign. Each earns on its own.
  3. Put your link on your site. The Referrer section shows it: https://testnet.bitcoin-arena.io/?ref=<your program's key>. A visitor who comes through it plays under your program for 30 days.
  4. Or host a game on your site. Copy the embed from the same section. Players play right on your page: 
    <iframe src="https://testnet.bitcoin-arena.io/?game=rock-paper-scissors&ref=<your key>"
        width="100%" height="720"></iframe>
    See it working on the demo page.
  5. Withdraw over Lightning. What your program earns is paid to its key in the game's federation, after each round settles. Your node collects it. In Referrer, press Withdraw and paste a Lightning invoice or a Lightning address.

Where you see it

Your node's Referrer section shows what each program earned and what it holds. The network's Metrics page and Health list every program by its name and what it earned.

Good to know

The program's name is signed with the program's own key, so nobody else can rename it. The 10% share is a network fee like the others: a passed fee proposal can change it for rounds after it takes effect.
